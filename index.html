Shorts Factory

A personal tool for turning long gameplay and stream videos into captioned vertical clips.

Shorts Factory runs on its owner's own computer. It finds highlight moments in videos the owner has the right to use, edits them into short vertical clips with captions and credit, and — after the owner approves each clip — uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel and social accounts.

It uses Google's YouTube Data API only to upload the owner's approved videos and to read basic statistics about those videos. It is not offered to other people and has no other users.
